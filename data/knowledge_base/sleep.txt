SLEEP AND WELLBEING

Sleep is an important part of physical and emotional wellbeing. Regular, sufficient sleep can support attention, learning, memory, mood, and daily functioning.

Poor sleep can be associated with tiredness, difficulty concentrating, irritability, and reduced ability to manage everyday stress. Stress and emotional difficulties can also make it harder to sleep, so sleep and wellbeing can influence each other.

Healthy sleep habits can include maintaining a reasonably consistent sleep and wake schedule, creating a comfortable sleep environment, allowing enough time for sleep, and following a relaxing routine before bedtime.

Screen use, caffeine, irregular schedules, stress, and environmental factors can affect sleep for some people. Individual sleep needs vary.

Persistent sleep difficulties that interfere with daily functioning may be worth discussing with a healthcare professional.

This document is intended for educational purposes and does not diagnose sleep disorders.
